abstract: When dealing with conformal prediction for real-world artificial intelligence
  applications, it is necessary to ensure its physical feasibility. In this work,
  we propose to tackle this problem for an autonomous wheelchair guided by a deep
  neural network for local navigation. We adapt the conformal sets to be compliant
  with the wheelchair’s kinematics, enhancing their efficiency while preserving coverage
  guarantees.
