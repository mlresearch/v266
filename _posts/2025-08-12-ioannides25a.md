title: Exponential Estimates for Contagion in Financial Networks
abstract: In financial systems contagion occurs when the default of institutions affected
  by a random shock triggers a domino effect, leading to defaults of other institutions
  within a given set. We provide a sufficient and necessary condition for weak contagion
  using exponential estimates for contagion and default probabilities resulting from
  Sub-Gaussian shocks.
