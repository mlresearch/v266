title: Robust Vision-Based Runway Detection through Conformal Prediction and Conformal
  mAP
abstract: We explore the use of conformal prediction to provide statistical uncertainty
  guarantees for runway detection in vision-based landing systems (VLS). Using fine-tuned
  YOLOv5 and YOLOv6 models on aerial imagery, we apply conformal prediction to quantify
  localization reliability under user-defined risk levels. We also introduce Conformal
  mean Average Precision (C-mAP), a novel metric aligning object detection performance
  with conformal guarantees. Our results show that conformal prediction can improve
  the reliability of runway detection by quantifying uncertainty in a statistically
  sound way, increasing safety on-board and paving the way for certification of ML
  system in the aerospace domain.
