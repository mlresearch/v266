title: Sequential Conformal Risk Control for Safe Railway Signaling Detection
abstract: 'As machine learning becomes a more common tool in industry, its needs for
  certification increase. Conformal Prediction, a framework for construction of prediction
  sets with tight coverage guarantees at any desired error rate, is an ideal tool
  for this purpose. However, adapting conformal methods to complex computer vision
  pipelines and providing appropriate guarantees is still a challenging task. Indeed,
  conformal approaches to object detection are often restricted to subtasks: often
  localization, and sometimes classification. In this study, we apply the comprehensive
  framework from (Andeol, 2025) to the safety-critical task of railway signaling detection.'
