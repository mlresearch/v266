title: 'Inductive randomness predictors: beyond conformal'
abstract: This paper introduces inductive randomness predictors, which form a proper
  superset of inductive conformal predictors but have the same principal property
  of validity under the assumption of randomness (i.e., of IID data). It turns out
  that every non-trivial inductive conformal predictor is strictly dominated by an
  inductive randomness predictor, although the improvement is not great, at most a
  factor of $e\approx2.72$ in the case of e-prediction. The dominating inductive randomness
  predictors are more complicated and more difficult to compute; besides, an improvement
  by a factor of $e$ is rare. Therefore, this paper does not suggest replacing inductive
  conformal predictors by inductive randomness predictors and only calls for a more
  detailed study of the latter.
